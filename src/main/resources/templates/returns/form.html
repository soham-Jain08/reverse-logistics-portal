What happens next?

  1. The return manager reviews and approves your request.
  2. A pickup date is scheduled for your address.
  3. The warehouse receives and inspects the item.
  4. You get a refund, or the item is restocked or disposed of.

Packing tips

  • Keep the original packaging if you can.
  • Include all accessories and manuals.
  • Print the return label once the request is approved.
