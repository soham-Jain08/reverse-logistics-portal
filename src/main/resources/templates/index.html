How it works

Four simple stages from request to resolution.

1

Request

The customer raises a return with the order, product, reason and pickup address.

2

Approve and schedule

The return manager approves the request and schedules the courier pickup.

3

Receive and inspect

Warehouse staff receive the item and record its condition.

4

Resolve

The item is refunded, returned to stock or disposed of, and the customer is updated.
